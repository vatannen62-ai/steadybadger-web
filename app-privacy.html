App privacy policy

Privacy in the Steady Badger app

Data controller: Miroslav Novotný, self-employed sole trader (OSVČ), Company ID (IČO) 04494407, registered address Jablonecká 360/21, 190 00 Prague 9 – Střížkov, Czech Republic; email vatannen62@gmail.com. Steady Badger is the app name and does not by itself identify the controller.

Information the app handles

Depending on what you enter, the app stores settings and profile information (for example language, age, sex, height, weight and movement options), workout and sports logs, goals and results, readiness and feedback including fatigue or pain, body measurements, workout templates and meal-planning information. Some information about mobility, the body or pain may be sensitive or reveal health information. Enter only information you want to use for your own records and app features. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions.

Storage and use

In the current version, the main records and up to 10 minimal error-event entries are stored locally in the app’s private storage on your device. Each error entry contains only an event identifier, a fixed category (framework/runtime) and a UTC timestamp; exception text, stack traces, user input and workout records are excluded. According to the current source review, the app has no user account, own data server or automatic workout-log synchronisation. The app does not provide a separately encrypted vault; protect your device with its lock and security settings. If Android backup is turned on in your phone, the system may automatically back up the app's data (up to 25 MB) to a private folder in your Google Account; on Android 9 or later with a screen lock, the backup is encrypted with your PIN, pattern or password. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings (usually Settings → System → Backup). The app has no access to this backup and does not manage it.

Voice and microphone

The microphone is used only after you start voice input and grant permission. The app first uses on-device speech recognition when available. Network fallback is off until you explicitly enable it in Settings after an explanation. If used, audio may be processed by the speech-recognition service configured on your phone under that provider’s own terms and settings. The app does not control that provider’s processing or retention. The app does not save an audio file; you review and confirm a recognized draft before it is saved. You can turn off network recognition.

Location, links, exports and sharing

A route may contain location and timestamped points. You explicitly start recording; according to the current data inventory, route records are stored on your device. The app does not use background location. When you open a link or video, the external service you choose may process data under its own terms. Example exercise videos load in the app from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you tap the video button; YouTube (Google) may then process, for example, your IP address, and the player may store cookies. The app itself sends YouTube no data from your workout log. You create or transfer a backup, report or card only through your action using the system file picker or share menu. On the error screen, you can also manually copy a short diagnostic report to the clipboard. Choose the destination and review the content before confirming. The app does not separately encrypt exported files; further copies and retention depend on the destination and your device settings.

Retention, deletion and your rights

You can edit or delete local records in the app, clear the app’s data in device settings, or uninstall the app. Diagnostic events are capped at the latest 10 and are removed when app data is cleared. Exported files and system backups may remain and must be removed separately. The app has no account through which the controller could restore or centrally delete your local workout log.

Send requests or questions to vatannen62@gmail.com. You may also contact the competent supervisory authority, in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

Changes to this policy

If data processing changes, for example by adding accounts, server synchronisation, analytics or a remote AI service, the app, Data safety information and this policy must be reviewed again. Effective date: 16 November 2026.

Coach mode

The coach mode MVP stores an 18+ declaration (without date of birth), invite code and per-category consent choices with their timestamps on the device. You transfer the invite manually as text. In this version, the app does not automatically send or sync workout, health or para data to a coach. The app does not independently verify the age declaration; you can disconnect the link at any time.
