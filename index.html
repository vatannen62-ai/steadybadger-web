03 / ACCESSIBILITY IS PART OF THE PLAN

More ways
to join in.

We are developing accessible controls alongside the training tools. Here is what is planned; we will confirm support after real-device testing.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Voice and screen readers

Spoken entry and reading aloud for blind users, with a clear confirmation step and manual input as a fallback. Offline Czech depends on the device.

PLANNED

Captions and visual cues

Readable exercise captions, a large countdown and optional vibration. Important instructions should work without sound.

LATER STAGE

Sign-language support

We want to explore sign-language support with Deaf users and qualified signers. It is not available yet; there is no single universal sign language.

A few useful answers.

Is the app available now?

Not publicly yet. We are developing and testing it; this page does not announce a release date or price.

Is it only for people with disabilities?

It is for people with and without disabilities. We focus on functional abilities and suitable exercise options rather than a single label.

Are the people in the images real?

The illustrative pictures and two virtual guides are AI-generated. Mirek is a real coach; we do not represent fictional people as real customers.

Which features can I use now?

There is no public release yet. The workout log, exercise library and accessible controls are in development and testing. Previews do not promise support on every phone.

Does voice input work offline?

It depends on the phone, language and recognition settings. We do not yet promise offline Czech or full voice control. Recognised values must be reviewed and confirmed first.

Who are MARK and Tessa?

MARK and Tessa are fictional AI characters. Mirek is a real Class III powerlifting coach (ČSST) and a wheelchair user.

What happens to my email?

We use it only to tell you about the app launch and DROP 01. You can ask us to delete it at any time. Details are in the privacy notice.

When can I buy DROP 01?

Not yet. We are testing samples now and will produce based on real interest. Join the list and you will hear first.
