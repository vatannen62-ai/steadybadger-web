Delete app data

The app has no user account

The current version of Steady Badger has no sign-in or server account. Your workout records are stored on your device, and the app controller cannot access them remotely. There is no online account to close and no copy of your log that we can delete for you.

Clear data on Android

  1. Open your phone’s Settings and go to Apps.
  2. Select Steady Badger and open Storage (or Storage & cache).
  3. Choose Clear data / Clear storage and confirm. This removes the app’s local records and settings.

Menu names may vary slightly by device maker and Android version. You can also uninstall the app; first decide whether you want to keep a manual export.

Also remove backups and exports

If Google Backup is enabled, Android may back up app data to your Google Account and restore it after a new installation. Check Google backup settings on your phone and any existing device backup. Manually exported files are not removed when app data is cleared: delete every copy from your phone, computer, cloud storage or other location where you saved it.

The app does not store audio files. If you enable network speech recognition for voice input, audio may be processed by your phone’s system speech service. Any retention or deletion by that provider is governed by its own settings and terms; the app cannot access or delete data held by the provider.
