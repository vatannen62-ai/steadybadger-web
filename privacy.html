This website is operated by Miroslav Novotný, Prague, Czech Republic (self-employed), who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: miroslavnovotny18@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

When you join the list we receive your email address, selected interest, optional T-shirt size, page language and page URL. The DROP 01 product form also includes your selected product, size, proposed colour and country. DROP 01 consent covers only DROP 01 updates and interest in items and sizes; the home-page choice covers the updates you select. Registration is not an order.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us; withdrawal does not affect earlier processing.

Who helps us

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technical data such as IP addresses in server logs. The signup form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our email inbox at Google (Gmail). These providers may process data outside the EU; they rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, and at most 24 months from signup.

Cookies and tracking

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may remember your “Motion on/off” choice locally; it never leaves your device.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent. Write to the email above. You may also complain to the Czech data protection authority, ÚOOÚ (uoou.gov.cz).
